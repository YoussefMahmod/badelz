Brand Identity

BADELZ LOGOS

Three distinctive concepts for Egypt's premier padel booking platform. Each designed for versatility across digital and physical touchpoints.

Concept 01

The Racket Grid

Athletic Geometric

The padel racket face — the most iconic element of the sport — abstracted into a clean geometric mark. The perforated grid pattern instantly communicates "padel" to anyone who's held a racket. A dynamic ball with motion trails adds energy and movement. The combination mark pairs a bold condensed wordmark with Arabic calligraphy in the brand's electric lime.

Concept 02

The Court Mark

Architectural Minimal

A bird's-eye view of a padel court becomes the brandmark. The clean court lines, service boxes, net, and four player positions create a recognizable symbol that speaks directly to the sport. The dashed outer boundary hints at the glass walls unique to padel. Works beautifully as an app icon — the court mark alone is instantly identifiable at any size.

Concept 03

The Smash Arc

Dynamic Premium

The signature padel smash — captured in a single fluid arc. The sweeping motion trail transitions from electric lime through emerald to teal, echoing the brand's gradient palette. A ball at the apex with radiating impact rings creates a focal point of energy. The secondary echo arc suggests follow-through and momentum. This mark conveys power, precision, and the thrill of the game.
