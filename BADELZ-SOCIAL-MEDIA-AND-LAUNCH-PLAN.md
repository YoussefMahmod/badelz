# PART 1: GO-TO-MARKET EXECUTION SUMMARY

This is the tactical layer on top of the full GTM strategy. It focuses on WHAT TO POST, WHEN, and WHERE -- the daily actions that turn strategy into results.

## The Three Phases at a Glance

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Weeks 1-2) -- "Build Trust, Build Waitlist"

**Goal**: 100+ waitlist signups, 5-8 venues ready, credibility established in communities.

**Your identity in communities**: A padel player who happens to be building something cool. NOT a startup founder pitching. The ratio should be 80% helpful community member, 20% "oh by the way I'm building this thing."

**Daily time commitment**: 1.5-2 hours/day

| Activity | Time | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Engage in Facebook groups (comment, answer questions, like posts) | 30 min | P0 |
| WhatsApp group participation (be helpful, share tips) | 15 min | P0 |
| Venue outreach (2-3 WhatsApp messages per day) | 30 min | P0 |
| Create 1 piece of content (post, graphic, reel) | 30 min | P1 |
| Follow up on venue conversations | 15 min | P1 |

**Key principle**: In pre-launch, your JOB is not to sell. Your job is to become a KNOWN FACE in the Egyptian padel community. When you eventually say "I built this," people should think "oh, that guy who's always helpful in the group -- cool, I trust him."

### Phase 2: Soft Launch (Weeks 3-4) -- "First Bookings, First Proof"

**Goal**: 30-50 bookings, 50-80 unique players, 10-15 venues live, Game Link viral loop seeded.

**Your identity shifts**: Now you're "the Badelz guy" -- but still a community member first. Lead with Game Links and invitations to play, not "download my app."

**Daily time commitment**: 2-2.5 hours/day

| Activity | Time | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Post Game Links in groups ("ناقصنا واحد") | 30 min | P0 |
| Respond to all bookings/feedback within 1 hour | 30 min | P0 |
| Content creation (reels, carousels, stories) | 45 min | P0 |
| Venue relationship management | 15 min | P1 |
| Community engagement (comments, answers) | 30 min | P1 |

### Phase 3: Growth (Weeks 5-8) -- "Flywheel Spinning"

**Goal**: 100+ bookings/week, 20+ active venues, 300+ registered players, organic growth visible.

**Your identity**: Badelz IS the padel community hub. You're the go-to source for court info, game invitations, and padel content in Egypt.

**Daily time commitment**: 2-3 hours/day (but much of it is now reactive -- responding to organic activity)

# PART 2: FACEBOOK GROUP POSTING PLAN

## The Golden Rules of Facebook Group Marketing in Egypt

1. **NEVER post in more than 2 groups per day.** Facebook's algorithm flags cross-posted content. And admins talk to each other.
2. **NEVER copy-paste the same text across groups.** Rewrite every post. Same idea, different words. Facebook detects duplicates.
3. **Post between 8-10 PM Egypt time (EET).** This is peak engagement. Sunday, Tuesday, and Thursday are the strongest days.
4. **Always end with a question.** Questions drive comments. Comments drive reach. Reach drives signups.
5. **Reply to EVERY comment within 1 hour.** This keeps your post in the feed. A post with 15 replies outperforms a post with 0 replies, even if the content is identical.
6. **Get admin permission.** If a group has rules about promotions, DM the admin first. Being banned kills your strategy dead.
7. **Be a member first, promoter last.** For every 1 promotional post, you should have 5+ non-promotional interactions (comments on other people's posts, answering questions, sharing tips).

## Your Posting Ratio (The 5:3:1 Rule)

For every 9 interactions in a Facebook group:
- **5 should be pure engagement**: Commenting on others' posts, answering questions, reacting. Zero mention of Badelz.
- **3 should be value-add content**: Polls, tips, padel knowledge, venue recommendations. Badelz is mentioned subtly or not at all.
- **1 should be direct promotion**: Game Links, venue announcements, booking invitations through Badelz.

# PART 3: WHATSAPP GROUP STRATEGY

## Rule #1: WhatsApp Groups Are Sacred

Facebook groups tolerate some self-promotion. WhatsApp groups DO NOT. If you post a link in a WhatsApp group that feels like an ad, you get muted or kicked instantly. Every message must add genuine value.

## How to Add Value Before Promoting (Weeks 1-2)

### Strategy: Be the "Organizer"

The person who organizes games in a WhatsApp group has STATUS. They're the one who says "who's playing Friday?" and everyone responds. Become that person in 2-3 WhatsApp groups.

**Week 1 actions**:
- Join 3-5 padel WhatsApp groups (found through Facebook posts and venue communities)
- Listen for 2-3 days. Understand the group dynamics. Who's the organizer? What gets posted?
- Start responding to "who wants to play?" messages: "انا available يوم الجمعة" (I'm available Friday)
- If no one is organizing, BE the one who asks: "حد عايز يلعب الخميس بالليل؟" (Anyone want to play Thursday night?)

**Week 2 actions**:
- Share useful info: "سمعت ان [Venue] عاملين خصم وقت الضهر. حد جرب؟" (Heard [Venue] has a daytime discount. Anyone tried it?)
- Help answer questions: If someone asks about a court, share what you know
- Organize 1 actual game through the group (YOU book the court, YOU collect the people)

## Message Templates for Different Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Sharing Badelz for the First Time in a Group

TIMING: Only AFTER you've been active in the group for 2+ weeks.

CONTEXT: Wait for a natural moment -- someone asking about booking, or complaining about coordination.

# PART 6: METRICS AND KPIs

## The Metrics That Matter (And The Ones That Don't)

### Vanity Metrics (Track But Don't Optimize For)
- Instagram followers
- TikTok views
- Facebook post reach
- Website page views

These feel good but don't correlate with success. A TikTok with 100K views and 0 bookings is worth less than a Facebook post with 50 views and 5 bookings.

### Real Metrics (Optimize These)

| Metric | What It Tells You | Target (30 days) | Target (60 days) | Target (90 days) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Total Bookings** | Are people using the core feature? | 30-50 | 150-300 | 500+ |
| **Unique Players** | Is the user base growing? | 50-80 | 200-350 | 700+ |
| **Active Venues** (received booking in last 7 days) | Is the supply side healthy? | 5-8 | 12-18 | 20-30 |
| **Lobby Creation Rate** | Are people using the social feature? | 15-25 | 60-100 | 200+ |
| **Game Link Share Rate** | Is the viral loop working? | 40%+ of lobbies shared | 50%+ | 60%+ |
| **Repeat Booking Rate** | Are people coming back? | 15-20% | 25-30% | 35%+ |
| **New Users from Game Links** | Is organic growth happening? | 10-20 | 50-100 | 200+ |
| **Waitlist-to-Active Conversion** | Did pre-launch work translate? | 30%+ | -- | -- |

|---|---|---|

### Week 3-4 (Launch)

| Metric | Target | RED FLAG (Below This = Problem) |
|---|---|---|
| Bookings (total) | 30-50 | Under 10 |
| Unique players | 50-80 | Under 20 |
| Lobbies created | 15-25 | Under 5 |
| Game Links shared | 10-15 | Under 3 |
| New users from shared links | 10-20 | Under 5 |
| Venue bookings distribution | At least 3 venues received bookings | All bookings at 1 venue |
| Repeat users (booked 2+ times) | 5-10 | 0 |

**If you hit RED FLAG territory**: Stop all marketing. Talk to users. Find out why they're not booking. Common causes:
1. Not enough venues (limited choice = no booking)
2. Booking flow too complicated (watch someone try to book -- time them)
3. No trust (people don't trust a new app with their phone number)
4. Venues not updating availability (players see "no slots" and leave)

- Venues ask YOU to join (inbound demand)

**What failure looks like at 90 days** (and what to do):
- Under 50 bookings/week and flat: The product or market assumption is wrong. Consider pivoting to a different angle (coach marketplace? Tournament platform? Venue management only?)

- Growth only when you post: The viral loop isn't working. Redesign the Game Link experience or the post-booking sharing flow
