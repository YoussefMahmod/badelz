## 1. Playtomic
**The gold standard. $273M valuation. 6,700+ clubs. 66 countries.**

### How They Charge VENUES (B2B)

**SaaS Subscription (Monthly):**
| Plan | Spain/EU | US | Features |
|------|----------|-----|----------|
| Standard | $59/mo | $119/mo | Bookings, chats, customers, reports, schedule, tournaments, 48hr support |
| Professional | $89/mo | $179/mo | + Court info display, user categories, wallet, POS, 8hr support |
| Champion (Popular) | $139/mo | $249/mo | + Coaches, courses, memberships, invoices, leagues, API, campaigns, 4hr support |
| Master | $279/mo | $349/mo | + Priority support |

- Annual billing discount available
- No free tier for venues
- Pricing varies by region (Spain, Mexico, UAE, US)

**B2B Commission:**
- Commission charged on every online booking processed through the platform
- Exact percentage NOT publicly disclosed (carefully guarded)
- Industry estimates: 5-15% per transaction (from Medium analysis)
- One source references ~EUR 0.20 per reservation as B2C component
- Commissions deducted in real-time from Stripe payouts
- Monthly invoice issued for B2B fees
- New billing model: funds deposited every 7 days (was 15 days)

### How They Charge PLAYERS (B2C)

**Service Fee:**
- 20% of match cost for open/public matches
- Minimum: GBP 0.24 per booking
- Maximum: GBP 0.89 per booking
- 4% booking fee on court reservations (avoidable by using wallet)
- No fee for private matches or tournaments

**Premium Player Subscription:**
- GBP 41.99/year (~GBP 3.50/month)
- Benefits: Zero booking fees, golden match promotion, advanced stats, cancellation notifications
- Premium users pay NO service fee (key lock-in mechanism)

### Revenue Numbers
- 2024 gross revenue: EUR 260M+ (EUR 240M transacted, 40% YoY growth)
- 2025 projection: EUR 350M gross, EUR 31M net revenue
- Net revenue per club: ~EUR 361/month (~EUR 4,330/year)
- Revenue mix estimate: SaaS 40-50% | B2B commission 30-40% | B2C service fee 15-20%
- Take rate: ~8% of gross transaction volume
- Processes up to EUR 14.5M in a single month
- 1.5M monthly active users, 200-300K daily users
- EUR 70M raised (March 2025), EUR 250M valuation

### Key Strategic Insight
Playtomic uses a **hybrid model**: SaaS subscription + commission + player fee. The genius is the player wallet that eliminates the 4% fee -- it locks money into the ecosystem. Premium subscription removes ALL fees, creating a tiered player experience.

## 2. Padel Finder (Egypt)
**Egypt's incumbent. 82K+ downloads. Built by Rami Selim (solo dev).**

### How They Charge VENUES

**Venue Listing Fees:**
- Confirmed: venues pay a fee to be registered/listed on the app
- Exact fee NOT publicly disclosed
- Estimated: 3,000-7,000 EGP/month per venue listing (reverse-engineered)

**White-Label App Licensing:**
- 11 confirmed white-label venue apps (SR Padel, etc.)
- Each venue gets its own branded booking app powered by Padel Finder backend
- Estimated: 3,000-7,000 EGP/month per white-label license
- Total white-label revenue estimated: 33,000-75,000 EGP/month

### How They Charge PLAYERS

**Mandatory Deposits:**
- Players required to pay deposits to book courts
- This is the #1 user complaint -- frequently called "greedy" in reviews
- Deposit amount and processing fee unknown but significant enough to generate resentment

**App itself is free to download**

### How They Charge COACHES
- No confirmed coach-specific monetization
- Has coach directory/marketplace functionality

### Revenue Estimate
- Total: 78,000-165,000 EGP/month (~$1,560-3,300 USD)
- Streams: White-label (~60%) + Listing fees (~25%) + Deposits (~15%)
- Solo dev income = 3-6x average Egyptian developer salary

### Key Vulnerabilities
1. Mandatory deposits = massive user resentment (Badelz advantage: no deposit)
2. White-label model has a ceiling (limited # of venues willing to pay for custom app)
3. English-first despite Egyptian market (Badelz advantage: Arabic-first)
4. Native app only (Badelz advantage: PWA = instant access)
5. No community features (open games, player matching)
6. No coach booking marketplace

## 3. MATCHi
**Swedish. 1,400+ venues in 15 countries. Acquired by PlayMore Group.**

### How They Charge VENUES

**Pricing: NOT publicly disclosed (quote-based)**
- Contact sales for custom pricing
- Believed to be subscription-based with possible transaction fees
- Offers "free" basic tier for small venues to get started (unconfirmed)

**Known Fee Components:**
- GBP 0.25 per player split payment fee (when players split court cost in-app)
- EUR 5/court/month for competition management features
- Digital signage solution: monthly subscription (price unknown)
- Padel kiosk: upfront purchase or monthly rental

### How They Charge PLAYERS
- Free to use the app
- Split payment feature: players who use split pay are charged GBP 0.25 each
- No confirmed premium player subscription

### Revenue Numbers
- Owned by PlayMore Group (Verdane-backed)
- Exact revenue not disclosed
- 1,400+ venues across 15 countries
- Strong in Sweden, UK, expanding globally

### Key Strategic Insight
MATCHi pursues a land-and-expand strategy: get venues on free/cheap basic tier, then upsell hardware (kiosks), premium features, and digital signage. Revenue from per-player split fees is elegant -- charges the friction-reducer, not the booking itself.

**Generic space booking. Used by Pro Padel Egypt and others.**

|------|-------|--------|-------------|

| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | + Dedicated manager, legal flexibility |

- Extra admin: $99/mo add-on
- Priced per space (court = 1 space)
- No commission model -- pure SaaS

### AllBooked by Skedda (Sports/Community Focus) Pricing

| Plan | Price | Spaces | Key Features |
|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| Core | $99/mo (annual) | 25 | Basic booking engine, limited rules, payments |
| Business | $149/mo (annual) | 25 | + Unlimited rules, add-ons, integrations |

Skedda/AllBooked is a generic tool, not padel-specific. Pro Padel Egypt uses it, proving Egyptian venues are willing to pay ~$100-200/mo for booking software. But it lacks: player discovery, open games, coach marketplace, community features. This is Badelz's wedge -- padel-specific at a fraction of the cost.

**US-based. Tennis/pickleball/padel. Thousands of clubs.**

### Pricing

| Plan | Price | Key Features |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Start | $99/mo | Basic club management |
| Grow | ~$199/mo (est.) | Advanced features |
| Scale | ~$299/mo (est.) | + Branded mobile app |
| Enterprise | Custom | Multi-location, network |

- All plans include: unlimited courts, memberships, instructors
- Annual billing discount available
- 30-day free trial
- One example: $329/mo billed annually for 16 courts (Scale tier)

- Pure SaaS model

### Key Strategic Insight
CourtReserve proves the "unlimited everything" SaaS model works -- no nickel-and-diming on court count or instructor count. Their $99-299/mo range is the US benchmark. For Egypt, this translates to roughly 1,000-3,000 EGP/mo range (which Badelz Business at 999 EGP/mo undercuts significantly).

## 6. Yalla Hagz (Egypt)
**Egyptian-made. Football + Padel. Arabic-first.**

### How They Work
- Separate apps: player app + venue owner admin app ("Yalla Hagz Court Owners")
- First football booking app in Egypt, expanded to padel/padbol
- Filter courts by location, availability, price
- Equipment rental and coaching sessions listed

### Pricing Model
- **NOT publicly disclosed**
- Likely commission-based on bookings (standard for MENA sports apps)
- Venues register through the admin app
- App is free for players
- Monetization believed to be: venue listing fees + booking commission

### Key Strategic Insight
Yalla Hagz validates that Egyptian venue owners will use a dedicated booking app. But it's football-first with padel as secondary -- Badelz being padel-first is a meaningful positioning advantage. Yalla Hagz also proves Arabic-first booking UX works in Egypt.

- "Commission-free" is becoming a competitive weapon

## 10. Badelz Positioning Analysis

### How Badelz Compares

| Dimension | Playtomic | Padel Finder | Skedda | Badelz |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|--------|--------|
| **Venue cost** | $59-279/mo + commission | Listing fee + white-label | $99-199/mo | FREE (0 EGP) -> 999 EGP |
| **Player cost** | 4% fee + 20% open match | Mandatory deposit | N/A | 15 EGP flat (optional) |
| **Commission on bookings** | 5-15% (est.) | Via deposits | None | None (Phase 0-1) |
| **Free tier** | No | No | No | Yes (core features) |
| **Arabic-first** | No | No | No | Yes |
| **Coach marketplace** | In Champion plan | Basic directory | No | Built-in from day 1 |
| **Community features** | Open matches | No | No | Lobby (open games) |
| **Padel-specific** | Yes (multi-sport) | Yes | No (generic) | Yes (padel-only) |
| **Payment model** | Online-first | Deposit-first | N/A | Pay at venue (cash) |
| **Egypt-optimized** | No | Partially | No | Fully |

### Badelz's Unfair Advantages in Pricing

1. **Free Forever Core:** No other padel platform offers a genuinely free tier for venues. This is the PLG wedge.

2. **No Commission (Phase 0-3):** While Playtomic takes 5-15% and Padel Finder demands deposits, Badelz sends 100% of revenue to the venue. The "zero commission" message is proven to resonate (Book & Go, Bookla).

3. **Flat Fee > Percentage:** The 15 EGP convenience fee is psychologically easier than "5% of your booking." At a 400 EGP court rental, 5% = 20 EGP vs. Badelz's 15 EGP. And the player knows exactly what they'll pay upfront.

4. **EGP Pricing:** Every competitor prices in EUR or USD. Badelz prices in EGP -- eliminating FX anxiety and signaling "built for Egypt."

5. **Cash-First Economy Fit:** "Pay at venue" is the default, not an afterthought. This matches how 80%+ of Egyptian transactions happen. Online payment is an upgrade, not a requirement.

### Pricing Gaps / Opportunities Identified

1. **Coach Monetization Gap:** Only Playtomic Champion (EUR 139/mo) includes coach management. Most platforms ignore coaches entirely. Badelz's free coach listing + Coach Pro (149 EGP/mo) addresses an underserved segment at 1/30th the cost of the Playtomic tier needed.

2. **Community Pricing Gap:** No platform charges specifically for community features (open games, player matching). These are either free or bundled into expensive tiers. Badelz's Player Pro (49 EGP/mo) could be positioned as "the social padel experience upgrade."

3. **Egyptian Price Sensitivity:** At 999 EGP/mo (~$20/mo), Badelz Business is 3-5x cheaper than global competitors' entry tiers ($59-119/mo). Yet Egypt's venue owners currently pay Padel Finder estimated 3,000-7,000 EGP/mo for less functionality.

4. **Volume-Based Pricing Missing:** Only Bookeo and SuperSaaS use booking-volume pricing. For Egypt's cash-heavy economy, a "pay per booking processed online" model could work alongside the flat subscription -- but only after building volume.

### Risk Assessment

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Race to bottom on pricing | High | Differentiate on features (Arabic, community, coaches), not price alone |
| Playtomic enters Egypt | Medium | They price in EUR, require online payment -- cultural mismatch |
| Padel Finder drops deposits | Medium | Badelz moat is community + Arabic UX, not just "no deposit" |
| Venues see "free" as "no support" | Medium | White-glove onboarding for founding venues, case studies |
| Players won't pay 15 EGP fee | Low | Fee is optional (pay at venue = always free), 15 EGP < cup of coffee |
