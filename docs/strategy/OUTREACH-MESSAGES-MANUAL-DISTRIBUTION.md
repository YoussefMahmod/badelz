## 1. Facebook Group Posts (Padel Egypt -- 67K members)

### 1A. "Looking for Players" Lobby Post

**Strategy**: Create an actual lobby on Badelz for a real game you intend to play. Post it in the group like any other player looking for partners. This is NOT fake -- you are genuinely organizing a game. The Badelz link is just how people join.

**When to post**: Thursday evening (people plan Friday/Saturday games) or Tuesday (midweek game planners)

```
حد فاضي بادل يوم الجمعة الصبح؟

عندنا كورت محجوز في [اسم الملعب] -- [المنطقة]، الساعة ١٠ الصبح.
ناقصنا ٢ لاعبين. المستوى متوسط/فوق المتوسط.
الكورت ب [٤٠٠] جنيه -- يعني ١٠٠ على الواحد.

لو عايز تيجي، سجل اسمك هنا عشان نعرف العدد:
badelz.app/play

أو ابعتلي رسالة هنا. أول ٢ هيبعتوا هياخدوا المكان.
```

**English translation:**
> Anyone free for padel this Friday morning?
>
> We have a court booked at [Venue Name] -- [Area], 10 AM.
> Need 2 more players. Intermediate/upper-intermediate level.
> Court is [400] EGP -- so 100 per person.
>
> If you want to come, put your name down here so we know the count:
> badelz.app/play
>
> Or message me here. First 2 to respond get the spot.

**Why this works**: It looks identical to the 20+ "looking for players" posts that appear in this group daily. The only difference is that instead of "DM me," you link to a Badelz lobby. People see the value (organized, clear info, easy to join) without feeling sold to.

**Variations -- rotate these weekly:**

```
حد يلعب بادل بكرة العصر؟

محجوزين كورت في [الملعب] الساعة ٥. محتاجين واحد كمان.
مبتدئ أو متوسط -- الموضوع ترفيهي مش بطولة 😄
١٠٠ جنيه على الواحد.

سجل هنا: badelz.app/play
```

> Anyone for padel tomorrow afternoon?
> Court booked at [Venue] at 5 PM. Need one more.
> Beginner or intermediate -- this is fun, not a tournament.
> 100 EGP per person.
> Sign up here: badelz.app/play

```
ماتش بادل السبت -- التجمع

ناقصنا لاعب رابع. الكورت في [الملعب] الساعة ٩ الصبح.
لو حابب تيجي ادخل سجل اسمك:
badelz.app/play

أول واحد يسجل ياخد المكان.
```

> Saturday padel game -- New Cairo.
> Need a 4th player. Court at [Venue] at 9 AM.
> If you want in, put your name down: badelz.app/play
> First to register gets the spot.

### 1B. "Venue Recommendation" Reply Template

**Strategy**: Monitor the group daily. When someone posts "where can I play in [area]?", reply with genuinely helpful info AND a Badelz link. Be the most helpful person in the comments, not the most promotional.

**When to use**: Every time someone asks "فين ملعب بادل في [منطقة]؟" or "حد يعرف مكان كويس في [منطقة]؟"

**Template 1 -- Specific venue recommendation:**

```
[اسم الملعب] في [المنطقة] كويس -- الكورتات نضيفة والأسعار معقولة (من [١٥٠] جنيه الساعة).

لو عايز تشوف كل الملاعب اللي في [المنطقة] ومواعيدها وأسعارها: badelz.app/browse

فيه [٥] ملاعب هناك تقريبا.
```

**English translation:**
> [Venue Name] in [Area] is good -- courts are clean and prices are reasonable (from [150] EGP/hour).
> If you want to see all the venues in [Area] with their hours and prices: badelz.app/browse
> There are about [5] venues there.

**Template 2 -- When you don't know the specific area well:**

```
ممكن تشوف الملاعب اللي في [المنطقة] هنا: badelz.app/browse

فيه أسعار ومواعيد كل ملعب. لو لقيت حاجة كويسة قولنا عشان الكل يستفيد!

> You can check the venues in [Area] here: badelz.app/browse
> It has prices and hours for each venue. If you find a good one, let us know so everyone benefits!

**Template 3 -- When someone complains about a venue:**

```
جرب [ملعب تاني] -- أنا بلعب هناك وكويسين.

لو عايز تقارن أسعار كل الملاعب في المنطقة: badelz.app/browse

> Try [Other Venue] -- I play there and they're good.
> If you want to compare prices of all venues in the area: badelz.app/browse
> Might help you find something better.

### 1C. "Community Game" Organizing Post

**Strategy**: Partner with a venue to get a discounted off-peak slot (Tuesday 2 PM, or similar dead hours). Organize a real community game. This is a relationship-building move -- you meet players AND demonstrate value to the venue owner.

**When to post**: Monday (to fill a Wednesday slot) or Wednesday (to fill a Friday morning slot)

```
ماتش بادل مفتوح -- [اسم الملعب]، [المنطقة]

يا جماعة، منظم ماتش بادل يوم [الأربع] الساعة [٢ العصر] في [اسم الملعب].

التفاصيل:
- المستوى: مبتدئ لمتوسط (الموضوع ترفيهي)
- السعر: [٧٥] جنيه بس على الواحد (سعر خاص)
- العدد: ٤ لاعبين (أول ٣ يسجلوا بعدي)

المكان كويس والكورتات indoor. لو حد مبتدئ وعايز يجرب -- ده الماتش المناسب، مفيش ضغط.

سجل اسمك هنا عشان تاخد مكانك:
badelz.app/play

لو العدد زاد عن ٤، نفتح كورت تاني.
```

**English translation:**
> Open padel match -- [Venue Name], [Area]
>
> Organizing a padel game on [Wednesday] at [2 PM] at [Venue Name].
>
> Details:
> - Level: Beginner to intermediate (it's for fun)
> - Price: Just [75] EGP per person (special rate)
> - Players: 4 (first 3 to register after me)
>
> Nice place, indoor courts. If you're a beginner wanting to try -- this is the right game, no pressure.
>
> Put your name down to secure your spot: badelz.app/play
>
> If we get more than 4, we'll open a second court.

**Why this works**: You are organizing a genuine event. The venue gets foot traffic during dead hours (which builds your relationship with them). Players get a discounted game. You get real users experiencing the lobby and booking flow. Everyone wins.

### 1D. "Just Played" Post-Game Recap

**Strategy**: After hosting a game (especially through a lobby), post a casual recap with a photo. This is social proof. It shows the community that real people are using the app to organize real games.

**When to post**: Same day as the game, within 2-3 hours of finishing.

```
لعبنا ماتش حلو النهارده في [اسم الملعب] 🏆

كنا ٤ -- اتنين منهم اتعرفت عليهم من هنا من الجروب.

لو عايز تلعب الأسبوع الجاي -- هنفتح ماتش تاني إن شاء الله.
تابعوا اللوبي هنا: badelz.app/play

شكرا [اسم اللاعب ١] و[اسم اللاعب ٢] على الماتش!
```

**English translation:**
> Played a great match today at [Venue Name]
>
> We were 4 -- two of them I met through this group.
> Courts were solid and prices were fair.
>
> If you want to play next week -- we'll open another game inshallah.
> Follow the lobby here: badelz.app/play

> Thanks [Player 1] and [Player 2] for the game!

**Key details:**
- Always tag the players you played with (if they consent) -- their friends see it
- Include a photo: group photo on court, or a shot of the courts. Do NOT include an app screenshot here -- this should feel organic
- If someone comments "I want to join next time" -- reply with the Badelz link immediately

**Variation -- shorter, more casual:**

```

## 2. WhatsApp Messages for Venue Owners

### 2A. Cold Outreach -- First Contact

**Strategy**: Keep it under 5 lines. Lead with THEIR benefit. Do NOT explain what Badelz is in detail -- just say enough to get a reply. The goal is a conversation, not a conversion.

**When to send**: Sunday to Thursday, 10 AM - 1 PM (slow hours for venues)

```
السلام عليكم يا [اسم الشخص/الملعب]

أنا [اسمك]، بلعب بادل في [المنطقة]. عندي أبلكيشن اسمه بادلز (badelz.app) بيساعد لاعبين يلاقوا ملاعب ويحجزوا.

حبيت أضيف [اسم الملعب] -- مجاني ومفيش عمولة. اللاعبين بيدفعوا عندكم في الملعب.

ينفع أعدي عليكم أوريكم الموضوع في ٥ دقايق؟
```

**English translation:**
> Assalamu alaikum [Name/Venue]
>
> I'm [Your Name], I play padel in [Area]. I have an app called Badelz (badelz.app) that helps players find venues and book.
>
> I'd like to add [Venue Name] -- it's free with no commission. Players pay you at the venue.
>
> Can I swing by and show you in 5 minutes?

**Why this structure:**
- Line 1: Greeting with their name (personal)
- Line 2: Who you are + credibility (you play padel locally)
- Line 3: The ask + the hook (free, no commission)
- Line 4: Call to action (in-person visit, not "check my link")

**If they respond "what's in it for us?" -- follow up with:**

```
ببساطة -- لاعبين جداد بيلاقوكم على الأبلكيشن ويحجزوا عندكم مباشرة. زي ما لو حد عملكم إعلان مجاني.

مفيش أي تكلفة. مفيش عمولة. الفلوس كلها بتيجيلكم.

الموضوع لسه جديد بس بنكبر بسرعة -- والملاعب اللي بتنزل دلوقتي هتبقى أول اللي اللاعبين يشوفوها.
```

> Simply -- new players find you on the app and book directly. It's like someone running you a free ad.
>
> No cost. No commission. All the money comes to you.
>
> It's still new but growing fast -- the venues that list now will be the first ones players see.

### 2B. Follow-Up After In-Person Visit

**Strategy**: Send this within 2 hours of visiting. Include their venue link on Badelz so they can see their own page live. This is the "wow" moment.

```
يا [اسم]، شكرا على وقتك النهارده! المكان فعلا جميل.

ضفت [اسم الملعب] على بادلز -- دي صفحتكم:
badelz.app/venues/[id]

لو فيه أي حاجة عايز تعدلها (أسعار، صور، مواعيد) -- قولي وأنا أظبطها فورا.

هبدأ أوجه لاعبين من الجروبات عليكم إن شاء الله. أول ما حد يحجز من عندنا هبلغك.
```

**English translation:**
> [Name], thanks for your time today! The place is really nice.
>
> I've added [Venue Name] to Badelz -- here's your page:
> badelz.app/venues/[id]
>
> If there's anything you want to change (prices, photos, hours) -- tell me and I'll fix it immediately.
>
> I'll start directing players from the groups to you inshallah. As soon as someone books through us, I'll let you know.

**Critical follow-through**: You MUST actually send players to this venue. If you promise traffic and don't deliver, you lose the relationship. Prioritize this venue in your next lobby post.

**Strategy**: After your first lobby or booking sends a player to their venue, message the owner immediately. This is the single most powerful message in this entire document. Proof of value converts skeptics into advocates.

```

لو حبيت أعمل بوست في جروب البادل عن ملعبكم -- قولي وأنا أنزله.

> [Player 1] and [Player 2] -- if they showed up, let me know so I can make sure everything went well.

**If they respond positively, escalate with:**

ممتاز! لو حابب أعملك حاجة:

> 1. Post about [Venue] in the Padel Egypt group (67K members)
> 2. Add better photos to your Badelz page
> 3. Create a special offer for followers (e.g., 10% off first booking)
>
> Which of these would you like to start with?

## 3. WhatsApp Messages for Coach Outreach

### 3A. Cold Outreach to a Coach (Found on Instagram)

**Strategy**: Coaches want students. That's the job-to-be-done. Lead with that. Don't explain the app -- explain what it does for THEM.

```
السلام عليكم كابتن [اسم]

شفت بروفايلك وأنت بتدرب بادل -- ماشاء الله مستواك حلو.

أنا عامل بادلز (badelz.app) -- أبلكيشن بادل في مصر فيه دليل مدربين. اللاعبين بيدوروا على كوتشات في منطقتهم وبيتواصلوا معاهم مباشرة.

حبيت أضيفك على الدليل -- مجاني طبعا. محتاج منك بس:
- المنطقة اللي بتدرب فيها
- سعر الحصة
- رقم الواتساب أو رقم التواصل

ده اللينك لو حبيت تشوف الكوتشات اللي موجودين: badelz.app/coaches

لو مهتم ابعتلي البيانات دي وأنا أضيفك النهارده.
```

**English translation:**
> Assalamu alaikum Captain [Name]
>
> Saw your profile and your padel coaching -- mashallah, your level is great.
>
> I've built Badelz (badelz.app) -- a padel app in Egypt with a coach directory. Players search for coaches in their area and contact them directly.
>
> I'd like to add you to the directory -- free of course. I just need:
> - The area where you coach
> - Session price
> - WhatsApp or contact number
>
> Here's the link if you want to see the coaches already listed: badelz.app/coaches
>
> If you're interested, send me those details and I'll add you today.

**Key notes:**
- Use "كابتن" (Captain) -- this is how coaches are addressed in Egypt. It shows respect.
- Open with a genuine compliment about their content -- not generic flattery.
- "مجاني طبعا" (free of course) is critical. Coaches are wary of platforms that take cuts.
- The ask is low-friction: just 3 pieces of info.

**If they ask "how many players are on the app?":**

```
لسه بنكبر بصراحة -- بس الأبلكيشن بينتشر في جروبات البادل (Padel Egypt فيه ٦٧ ألف عضو) ولاعبين بيدوروا على كوتشات كل يوم.

اللي بيسجل دلوقتي بيبقى أول واحد يطلع في البحث -- يعني لما اللاعبين يزيدوا، أنت أول واحد يشوفوك.

مفيش أي خسارة -- مجاني وبياخد دقيقة.
```

> Honestly, we're still growing -- but the app is spreading in padel groups (Padel Egypt has 67K members) and players search for coaches every day.
>
> Whoever registers now will be the first to appear in search -- so when players increase, you're the first one they see.
>
> Nothing to lose -- it's free and takes a minute.

## 5. Execution Rules

### Daily Routine (30 minutes/day)

**Morning (10 AM -- 15 min):**
1. Open Padel Egypt Facebook group
2. Sort by "New"
3. Reply to the first 3-5 relevant posts using templates above
4. Check if any venue owners or coaches replied on WhatsApp

**Afternoon (3 PM -- 10 min):**
1. Check group again for new posts
2. Reply to 2-3 more
3. Send any pending WhatsApp follow-ups

**Evening (8 PM -- 5 min):**
1. Quick scan for "tomorrow morning" game posts
2. Reply to 1-2 if relevant
3. If you're playing tomorrow, create your lobby and post it

### Weekly Rhythm

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Saturday | Play a game. Post recap after (Template 1D). |
| Sunday | Visit 1 venue in person. Send follow-up WhatsApp (Template 2B). |
| Monday | Reply to 5+ group posts. Post community game for Wednesday (Template 1C). |
| Tuesday | DM 2 venue owners cold (Template 2A). DM 1 coach (Template 3A). |
| Wednesday | Host the community game. Share results with venue (Template 2C). |
| Thursday | Post lobby for Friday/Saturday game (Template 1A). Heavy group engagement. |
| Friday | Day off OR play another game and post recap. |

### Tracking

For every action, log:
- What you posted/sent
- Who responded
- Did it convert to a signup, lobby join, or booking?
- Which template worked best?

**The ONE metric that matters this week: completed bookings where a player showed up at a venue.**

Not signups. Not visits. Not impressions. Actual butts on padel courts through Badelz.

### Do NOT

- Post more than once per day in the same group (you'll look like spam)
- Use the same template twice in the same group within a week
- Send bulk WhatsApp messages (one-by-one only)
- Oversell or make promises about traffic you can't deliver
- Post without a real game/lobby behind it (hollow posts get called out fast)
- Use formal Arabic -- keep it Egyptian colloquial always
- Mention competitors by name (ever)
- Say "download" -- it's a PWA, just say "ادخل على badelz.app"

### DO

- Reply to other people's posts FIRST, then post your own content
- Be a genuine, helpful community member 90% of the time, link to Badelz 10%
- Follow up personally with anyone who clicks a lobby link but doesn't complete registration
- Ask venue owners you visit to mention Badelz to walk-in players
- Play actual padel games organized through the app -- nothing beats eating your own cooking
- Screenshot real bookings/lobbies to share as social proof
- Thank players publicly after games (builds goodwill + visibility)
