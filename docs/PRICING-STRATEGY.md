# Badelz Pricing Strategy

## Core Model: Free Launch → Deposit Monetization (Option C)

### Phase 1: Pure Free (Month 1-2)
- Everything free for players and venues
- No fees, no deposits, no commission
- Goal: 15 venues, 100 bookings/week, prove Game Link viral loop

### Phase 2: Introduce Deposits (Month 3)
- Players pay ~100 EGP refundable deposit when booking
- Deposit refunded when they show up at the venue
- No-show → venue keeps deposit, Badelz takes 10%
- Positioned as player benefit: "Guarantee your spot"
- Payment via Fawry / VCash / InstaPay (Egyptian payment methods)

### Phase 3: Split Deposits via Game Link (Month 4)
- Game Link sends 4 deposit links (25 EGP each)
- All 4 must pay deposit = game confirmed
- Combines virality (forced app interaction) with monetization
- Reduces no-show rate dramatically (money committed = people show up)

### Phase 4: Premium Tiers (Month 7+)
- **Badelz Pro (Players)**: 49 EGP/month
  - Priority booking (see slots 30 min before others)
  - Unlimited "Fill My Spot" requests
  - Player stats and match history
  - No-show protection (insurance)

- **Badelz Business (Venues)**: 999 EGP/month
  - Analytics dashboard (peak hours, revenue trends, player demographics)
  - Dynamic pricing suggestions
  - Featured placement in browse
  - WhatsApp marketing tools
  - Multi-staff access
  - No-show protection tools

### Revenue Projections
| Phase | Monthly Revenue |
|-------|----------------|
| Month 1-2 | 0 EGP |
| Month 3-4 | 3,000-6,000 EGP (deposit commissions) |
| Month 5-6 | 10,000-15,000 EGP |
| Month 7-9 | 20,000-30,000 EGP (deposits + premium tiers) |
| Month 10-12 | 50,000-70,000 EGP |
| Year 2 | 200,000-400,000 EGP/month |

### Key Principle
Never charge for something that adds friction without value. The deposit solves a REAL problem (no-shows) for BOTH sides — that's why it works as a revenue model.
